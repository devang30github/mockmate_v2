Before you start

  • Your browser will ask to use your microphone. You answer out loud; there is no typing.
  • Your voice is sent to this MockMate server while you answer and turned into text. The raw audio is held in memory only for transcription and is not saved.
  • The text of the questions and your answers is saved to your account, so you can reload this page and continue.
  • When the interviewer speaks, the text of each question, which can mention details from your resume, is sent to Sarvam AI's speech service to be turned into audio.
  • Headphones work best. The report is practice feedback, not a hiring decision.
